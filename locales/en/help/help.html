Plugin activation
Check this box to put the public side of the blog in construction mode. Visitors then see the message instead of the blog. The administration is not affected.
Allowed IP
One IP address per line. These visitors see the blog normally. Add your own IP address, shown under the field, to check your blog while it is in construction mode.
Extra allowed URL types
Types of public pages that stay available for everybody, separated by commas, for example feed to keep the syndication feeds. The contact page is always available.
Title
Title of the construction page.
Message
Text of the construction page, in HTML.
